Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who is Pilates for?
Everyone — from complete beginners to advanced, and every fitness level. It’s especially helpful for desk workers with office syndrome, anyone with back pain or posture issues, and people in rehabilitation.
What ages do you teach?
From young adults to seniors. Every exercise is adapted to the person, their age and their condition.
Do I need any experience?
Not at all. We meet you where you are and progress at your own pace.
I have office syndrome — can Pilates help?
Yes, it’s one of our main focuses. We analyse your posture and use targeted exercises and breathing to relieve rounded shoulders, forward neck and back pain from long hours sitting.
I have scoliosis or a back condition. Is it safe?
Bee is certified in Scolio-Pilates, the method for scoliosis and postural conditions. With proper guidance we adapt every session to work safely with your body.
Private or group classes?
Both. Private and Duo sessions give fully tailored attention; group classes offer community and great value. Not sure which suits you? We’ll help you choose.
What should I wear or bring?
Comfortable clothing you can move in, and grip socks. We provide all the equipment.
Do you run health workshops?
Yes. Bealatriz hosts workshops with physiotherapists and doctors — for example on posture and office syndrome — because we’re a space to learn about your health, not only to train.
