Frequently asked questions

Is Pilates really for me?
Yes — and we truly mean it. Athlete or complete beginner, a busy parent, recovering from an injury, or someone who hasn’t moved in years: Pilates meets you exactly where you are today. You don’t need to “get fit” before you start — this is how you get there, gently, safely, with a teacher guiding every single movement. From your very first class, you belong here.
I’m not flexible or strong — can I still start?
That’s exactly why Pilates works — you don’t need flexibility or strength to begin, you build both here, one small step at a time. Most people are amazed how quickly what felt impossible starts to feel natural. Come exactly as you are; taking care of the rest is our job.
What ages do you welcome?
From your twenties to your seventies and beyond. Pilates is one of the rare practices that grows with you — building strength when you’re young, and protecting your joints, balance and posture as the years pass. Every exercise is tuned to your age and your body, so it always feels right for you.
I sit all day and my neck, shoulders and back ache. Can you really help?
This is one of the reasons Bealatriz exists. Long hours at a desk pull your shoulders forward, push your neck out and round your back — and stretching on your own rarely reaches the real cause. We read your posture, find what’s actually happening, and use precise exercises and breathing to release the tension and retrain your body. Clients often tell us they feel lighter and stand taller after just a few sessions — and that first “oh, this is how it should feel” moment is the one we live for.
I have scoliosis or a back condition. Is it safe for me?
Not only is it safe — it may be one of the best decisions you make for your body. Bee is certified in Scolio-Pilates, the specialised method for scoliosis and postural conditions, and we work hand-in-hand with physiotherapists and doctors. Every movement is chosen and adapted for your body, so you build strength and confidence without fear — surrounded by people who understand exactly what you need.
I’ve never done Pilates. Will I feel lost or judged?
Never. Your very first class is a warm, judgement-free welcome — we explain everything, guide every movement and stay right beside you. You’ll leave knowing exactly what you did and why it matters for your body. Most people walk out of that first session already feeling like this is their place.
Private or group — which is right for me?
It depends on what you’re looking for, and we’ll help you find it. Private and Duo sessions give you our full, personalised attention — ideal if you have a specific goal, an injury, or you simply love focused one-on-one time. Group classes bring energy, connection and great value. Many clients love mixing both. Tell us your goal and we’ll guide you to the perfect start.
What should I wear and bring to my first class?
Just comfortable clothing you can move freely in, and a pair of grip socks (we can help you get some). Bring water and an open mind — everything else, from reformers to mats, is here waiting for you. Come a few minutes early and we’ll settle you in with a smile.
How soon will I feel a difference?
Most people feel taller, looser and more connected to their body after the very first class. With two or three sessions a week, real change — less pain, better posture, a stronger core — usually shows within a few weeks. The secret isn’t punishing intensity, it’s consistency, and we’ll keep you motivated and cheering you on the whole way.
Is Bealatriz more than a workout?
Absolutely. We’re a Healthy Space, not just a studio. Alongside your classes we host workshops with physiotherapists and doctors — on posture, office syndrome and truly understanding your own body — because caring for your health starts with understanding it. When you join Bealatriz, you’re not buying a handful of sessions; you’re joining a community that looks after you, and giving a gift to your body and mind.
